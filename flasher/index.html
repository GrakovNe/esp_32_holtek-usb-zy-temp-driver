Air Monitor

Install the firmware

One button, no tools. The device then sets itself up from your phone.

You need
  • An ESP32-S3 board with two USB-C ports, such as the ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1. 4 MB of flash is enough.
  • A Holtek USB-zyTemp CO₂ sensor, the one inside the TFA AirCO2ntrol Mini and similar meters.
  • On the DevKitC-1, the USB-OTG solder jumper must be closed so the USB port powers the sensor. Boards with a USB power switch work out of the box.
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer. Firefox and Safari cannot talk to serial ports.
Install
  1. Connect the board's COM port (the one next to the UART chip, not the USB-OTG port) to the computer.
  2. Press the button, pick the serial port in the dialog, confirm. A fresh board takes about a minute.
This browser cannot access serial ports. Use Chrome or Edge, or flash with esptool below. Serial access needs HTTPS. Open this page over https://. Reinstalling keeps your settings and history unless you choose "erase".
Then
  1. Plug the sensor into the board's USB port. Power the board from the COM port.
  2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network AirMonitor-XXXX. The setup page opens by itself; if not, open http://192.168.4.1/.
  3. Pick your home Wi-Fi, enter the password, save. The device restarts and is reachable at http://airmonitor-xxxx.local/ or at the address your router gave it.
If the port does not show up
  • Windows may need the CH9102 or CP210x driver from the chip vendor; macOS and Linux have them built in.
  • Use a data cable, not a charge-only one.
  • Close other programs that may hold the port, such as a serial monitor.
Without a browser

The same image, airmonitor.bin, flashed with esptool:

esptool --chip esp32s3 -p PORT -b 115200 write-flash 0x0 airmonitor.bin

Or with Espressif Flash Download Tool on Windows: chip ESP32-S3, file airmonitor.bin at address 0x0, SPI mode DIO.
